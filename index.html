About

I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Science and Engineering, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen). Previously, I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from CUHK in 2025, supervised by Prof. Bei Yu. I received my B.Eng. from Zhejiang University (ZJU) in 2021. My research interests include 3D Integrated Circuits, VLSI CAD, Machine Learning in EDA, and GPU Acceleration.

I am seeking motivated M.Phil./Ph.D. students and Research Assistants with backgrounds in electronic engineering, computer science and engineering, applied mathematics, or related fields. If you are interested, please feel free to contact me by email.
